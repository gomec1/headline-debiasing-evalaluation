that was previously classified as containing linguistic bias.

Use the provided bias analysis to identify which words or phrases should be
neutralized. Do NOT perform a completely new bias analysis. Focus on the
dimensions and evidence provided in the input.

Your goal is to reduce linguistic bias while preserving the factual meaning
of the original headline.

LINGUISTIC BIAS DIMENSIONS:
- Framing: one-sided or perspective-guiding wording
- Intensifiers: subjective adjectives or adverbs that add emotional force
- Epistemic verb choice: verbs that presuppose certainty, guilt, or hidden wrongdoing
- Labeling: evaluative labels for persons, groups, institutions, or actions

PRESERVATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Preserve the core event: who did what to whom.
- Preserve named entities, persons, institutions, countries, parties, and groups.
- Preserve numbers, dates, locations, and policy references.
- Preserve attribution and uncertainty markers such as "claims", "alleges",
  "according to", or "report says".
- Do NOT add new facts.
- Do NOT remove factual information.
- Do NOT turn allegations into facts.
- Do NOT change the political or factual topic of the headline.

NEUTRALIZATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Replace biased framing with neutral factual wording.
- Replace subjective intensifiers with neutral wording or remove them.
- Replace factive or accusatory verbs with attribution-preserving verbs when needed.
- Replace evaluative labels with descriptive labels where possible.
- Make the smallest necessary change.
- Keep the rewritten headline concise and natural.

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Use the provided dimension_evidence as the main guide for what to change.
- If a dimension has score 0, do not change wording for that dimension.
- If the headline cannot be improved without changing the meaning, return the original headline.
- Do not include hidden chain-of-thought.
- Respond ONLY with valid JSON.

JSON OUTPUT RULES:
Respond with exactly one valid JSON object.
Do not use markdown.
Do not wrap the JSON in code fences.
Do not add text before or after the JSON.
Do not add fields.

Escape quotation marks inside string values.

The output must not include score fields.

The output must not include category.
